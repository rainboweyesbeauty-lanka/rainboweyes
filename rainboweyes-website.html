Every bloom starts as a straight wire, or a flat scrap of fabric.

There's no factory line here — just a spool of chenille stems, a stack of fabric petals, and a lot of evenings spent twisting, folding, and shaping until something looks right. RainbowEyes started with one simple idea: flowers don't have to wilt to mean something.

Every colour, shade, and bunch is picked to be seen and felt, not just looked at once and forgotten. If you don't see the exact shade you're after, tell us — most blooms can be made to match your story.

Simple by design

How to order

No accounts, no checkout forms — just three steps between browsing and having your blooms on the way.

1

Pick your blooms

Browse the shop above and tap "Add to order" on anything that catches your eye. Mix pipe cleaner and artificial freely.

2

Send your list

Your picks collect in the order bar at the bottom of the screen. One tap sends the full list straight to us on WhatsApp.

3

We craft & confirm

We'll confirm colours, pricing, and delivery or pickup with you directly, then get twisting and stitching.

Good to know

Colours, care, and custom orders

Custom colours

Have a shade in mind that isn't in the shop? Most designs can be twisted or stitched to match — just message us with a reference.

Caring for your blooms

Pipe cleaner flowers: keep dry and out of direct sun. Artificial flowers: dust gently with a soft brush every few weeks.

Delivery & pickup

Local pickup and island-wide courier delivery are both available. Options and timing are confirmed when you send your order.
